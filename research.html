ABSTRACT

This research investigates context-aware sentiment classification of social media text using a RoBERTa-based transformer framework. The study uses a dataset containing 18,170 YouTube comments and develops an end-to-end NLP pipeline involving text preprocessing, RoBERTa tokenization, transfer learning, weighted cross-entropy loss, and label smoothing.

The resulting model achieved an overall accuracy of 96%, a weighted F1-score of 0.96, and a macro F1-score of 0.95, demonstrating the effectiveness of transformer-based architectures for sentiment classification of noisy social media text.
